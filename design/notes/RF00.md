## 3 · Shared approach
- **`design/canvas/scripts/realdata.py`** (pure data, no HTML, imports nothing from sNN): reads the DATA03 extracts once and exposes
  `TODAY=date(2026,11,18)`, `PLACEMENT`, `age_days(d)`, `sapt(d)` (D151), `week_dates(n)` (Tue–Mon), `FARMS`, `HALLS`, `SECTIONS` (per farm),
  `OLD2NEW`, `alive(sec,sx,d)`, `day(d)`/`week(n)` (hall/farm/section rows), `deaths(d)` vs `sales(d)` (depop → Vânzare, D155), `lay_day(d)`/
  `lay_week(n)` (D156), feed/stock/deliveries, `guide_lay(n)`, `guide_feed(n,sx)`, `guide_growth(n)`, `weigh_guide(n)` (n−1, D157), `ILL`
  (illustrative-events) and Telița/prev-flock accessors (prev mapped to new codes). Formats stay in s4s (`n`, `num`, `rnd`, `dd`); s4s/s7 import
  realdata and keep their exported names (`S`, `CL`, `GL`, `TODAY`, `WK`, `SECTIONS`, `HALLS`) as aliases so downstream rows at least import.
- Figures still moving (D155 mortality split, D156 lay %) are **computed in realdata from the daily file**, never copied from key-figures md.
  realdata asserts only the stable key figures (alive 12 561/794, eggs 5 914, SAPT 59, arrivals 26 883/3 024).
- **Refactor row by row in README order** (each job runs its row and every row its scripts rewrite through imports). Byte-identical is
  impossible; the checks per job are: (1) `scripts/literal_scan.py` over `design/canvas/project/*.dc.html` and the touched scripts, failing on
  `\bH[134]\.[12]\b` and H1/H3/H4 in Hîrbovăț context, `16.11.2026` as today, `SAPT 40`, `10.02.2026`, `25 252`, `25 257`, `2 685`,
  `26 765`, `29 825`, `28 252`, `3 967`, `18 688`, `sample-hirbovat`, `cycle_week`, „− 18”, with an allowlist per board for D39 examples;
  scan rows already ported (a row not yet ported is reported, not failed); (2) idempotence: run twice → byte-identical; (3) rows with no data
  change (S1, S22, most of S21/S19 states) compare equal to the old output after the old→new substitution map; (4) each board's key
  figures asserted against realdata in the generator (as today's asserts, retargeted).

## 5 · Risks
- **Two roots, one import web**: s4s and s7 feed every row; renaming anything there breaks all rows. Keep the exported names; each
  later job must re-run the rows its scripts rewrite (README import chain): s7 ← s6/s7s/s2/s8/s9/s10/s13; s4t ← s14/s16; s2 ← s2s/s14s/s15;
  s14s ← s17 ← s18 ← s19 ← s20/s21/s22 (and the s19 /tmp boards.json note). S13 rewrites S7 boards, S6 rewrites S7 at 3162: fix the int first.
- Until a row's job lands it cannot be rebuilt at all (stale boards stay on the canvas); do not run later rows to "check" early.
- **Heights**: S4, S7, S11, S12, S13, S6, S9 are hand-placed (no canvas step) and S4/S7 heights are ints in README. S4N/S14N/S3N tables
  grow rows far past 120 px gaps; `--canvas` saves stop on overlap. RF01 adds `canvas_rows.py relayout` (row y = previous y + tallest h + 120,
  x untouched) run after every job; heights come only from `sNN_render.py` then `--canvas`, never typed by guess.
- sys10/sys08/sys09 run cleanly with stale examples: only the literal scan catches them. sys11 helpers are exec'd by s20 (S20N must port).
- Figures moving under DATA (D155/D156): jobs that start before DATA03 is final re-run after it; realdata keeps them in one place.
- Illustrative records (culls, sexing errors, weighings, authors, a correction) must come from illustrative-events-hirbovat.json so S6/S15/
  S16/S17/sys09 tell one story; no generator invents its own.
