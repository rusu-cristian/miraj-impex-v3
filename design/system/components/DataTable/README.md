# DataTable

Compact tables on `raised` with uppercase caption headers on `panel`.

- **Rows:** `row-h`, separated by `rule`. Numbers are right-aligned in Plex Mono.
- **Record-type columns:** the header carries the record icon in its colour, and ♀/♂ columns are coloured.
- **Groups:** `sunken` rows.
- **Totals:** a `line-strong` top border.
- **Inline edit cells** (owner prices) use a medium TextField, sized to the row (row detail below).
- **Phone:** horizontal scroll with the first column sticky.
- **Row headers (additions.css §32, FIN14):** `table.ip-table.ip-table--rowheads` for rows named by `th[scope=row]` (group rows `th[scope=rowgroup]`, a totals row in `tbody` or `tfoot`): body text 13/18 `ink` on `raised`, `row-h`; group th `sunken` 500, totals th 500 on `panel` with the `line-strong` edge; `th.ip-table__child` indents a child row `space-6`. Used by S3, S4, S11, S12, S14, S18–S22, CostBlock and the small-curve table.
- **Heads on one line (§32, SYS17, D189):** every head cell stays on one line (`.ip-table thead th{white-space:nowrap}`); a column is as wide as its head needs and a table wider than its box scrolls under the sticky first column (canvas boards grow wider, D167 4). The FIN14 opt-in `ip-table--wraphead` (figure heads wrapping between words) is retired. Where a phone group would not fit 358, the head shows a short word with the full word in `ip-sr` (PeriodTable › Phone).
- **Column hairlines and centred heads (§73, SYS19, D194):** every two adjacent columns are parted by a slight vertical hairline, `col-rule` (steel-900 at 10 %, 1.22–1.24:1 on raised, panel, sunken and every colgroup tint), through the head rows and the body: an inset shadow on each cell's end edge (no column widens, no row grows), none on a row's last cell, so a cell spanning the whole row (load-more row, detail row, group row, message row) has no inner line; a cell before a PeriodTable group or section edge leaves that edge to the stronger `colgroup-rule` / `line-strong`, and the sticky first column keeps its `rule` border (one line per boundary). Every head cell (group, leaf, sub-head, `th.num`) is centred over its column; body cells keep their alignment (figures right, text and row heads left). Forced colours: a 1px CanvasText border.
- **Scroll region (§36, FIN14):** `div.ip-tablescroll[role=region][tabindex=0][aria-label=the caption]` (+ `--framed` for the table's box): scrolls sideways in its own box, first column sticky with a `rule` edge; a first cell spanning columns does not stick; row states keep their fill on the first column. A loading copy is `aria-hidden`, not a tab stop.
- **Totals edge (§32, FIN14B):** a totals row (`tr.is-total`) after a body row, first in a following `tbody`, or in a `tfoot` has one `line-strong` top edge: the row above drops its own `rule` (separate-border tables drew both; collapsed ones showed only the rule). No screen or CostBlock copy.
- **Head icon (§32, FIN14B):** `span.ip-table__ico` before a caption head's words: a 14px icon on the 14px caption line, top-aligned, `space-1` before the words; with `ip-rec ip-rec--<type>` it is the record icon in its colour (record-type columns). Used by S3, S4, S11, S12, S14, S16, S17 and this preview.
- **Current row (S22P/FIN05, additions.css §45/§51):** the record open in a SidePanel beside the table: `tr.is-current` + `aria-current="true"` on the row's key (not drawn pressed); `sunken` with a hair `line-strong` frame inside, steel only, no lamp.
- **Row detail (FW03, D150, additions.css §69):** a row with an inline edit, hint, suggestion or error keeps the table's row height: its fields and keys sit on the row line in their own columns at row-h − space-1 (28), centred in `td.edit` without block padding (the edited row `tr.ip-table__edit` on `sunken`); a table whose rows are taller sets `--ip-row-field-h` (S11: `control-h`). Phone: a table with an edited row takes touch rows (52) throughout, fields and keys `control-h-touch`. Everything that made it taller (hint or suggestion, error line with the alert icon, extra fields such as Motiv, a journal note, Salvează/Anulează when they don't fit the row) goes in ONE detail row right under it: `tr.ip-table__detail > td[colspan=all] > div.ip-table__detailbody`, starting at the first column's text start, one wrapping line (message lines whole; a field line = TextField 384 + keys + aside note `p.ip-table__aside > span` taking the rest of the line, text left-aligned and vertically centred on the field's line; the field's error drawn under its line; figures in `ip-num` Mono). No rule between the two rows (the row's hair takes its fill, also in collapsed tables), the rule closes after the detail; same fill as its row; never sticky, never widens a column; in a scroll region it stays in view at the start; phone: keys and note wrap under the field. A11y: the field `aria-invalid` + `aria-errormessage` → error, `aria-describedby` → the detail's texts; the detail cell is a plain td (no headers); focus order row fields → detail fields → keys. Used by S11 modifică preț, S12 în lucru, S19 tree edit rows (SectionTree), S20 refused rows (GuideImportPreview); see the preview.
- **Period tables (SYS14, additions.css §71):** week rows with their days inside, column groups by record type and section, Detaliu switch, sticky head: see `PeriodTable/`.
