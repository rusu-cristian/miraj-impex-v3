# VoiceButton

The voice-command key (spec §8 F1 Comenzi vocale, D199): one key on every signed-in screen and state, the same place on every screen. additions.css §78 (VO01); spec components.md › Additions › VO00 / VO01.

- **Markup:** `button.ip-voicekey` + look: `--fab` (phone: round 56 = `count-input-h`, Lucide mic 22, `raised` + 1px `line-strong` + `shadow-overlay`: the recorded D199 exceptions to "round = lamps and the centre key" and to the overlay-shadow list) or `--head` (desktop: `control-h` square, mic 16, the IconButton's secondary look, `title` "Comandă vocală · Ctrl+Shift+Space", inside `span.ip-voiceanchor`, the last key of the PageHeader actions, alone where a page has none). The key holds the mic `svg.ip-icon` and the Button spinner `span.ip-spin`; `aria-busy` shows one of them, so a script only sets attributes.
- **A11y:** a toggle named "Comandă vocală": `aria-pressed` (true while listening and while the panel is open on a result), `aria-busy="true"` while thinking (name kept), `aria-keyshortcuts="Control+Shift+Space"`; no `aria-expanded` / `aria-controls`. The phone key has no tooltip: "Comandă vocală · Ctrl+Shift+Space" is its description (`aria-describedby` → `span.ip-sr`). Disabled = `aria-disabled="true"`, still focusable, the reason in `aria-describedby` ("Fără conexiune"); a press opens the panel on that reason. Targets 56 (phone), 32 (desktop, ≥24).
- **States:** rest · hover `sunken` (only under `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`) · press 0.97 (`--ip-press-scale`, 120) · latched (`control` + `on-control`; desktop drops its key shadow, the phone key keeps `shadow-overlay`) · thinking (latched + spinner) · disabled (`sunken`, `ink-disabled`, 1px `line`) · focus (the 2px `focus` ring, 2px offset, round on the phone key). No pulse, ring or level on the key: the state word and the meter are in the VoicePanel. Latch/unlatch = colour only, also under reduced motion. Forced colours: latched = Highlight.
- **Phone placement** (`.ip-voicekey--float`, a direct child of `div.ip-screen`; fixed in the app, absolute in a fixed board frame): right `space-4`, bottom `space-4` above the lowest bar (`--ip-voice-bar`: BottomTabBar `tabbar-h` + safe area; a SaveBar or the S16 sheet foot = touch key + `space-3` × 2 + hair), z 3: over content and bars, under a Toast, a sheet scrim and the Dialog scrim (inert there). The same element is re-parented: over a BottomSheet it is the scrim's first child (on the scrim, `space-4` above the sheet's top edge); with the VoicePanel open it docks in the panel's foot, and the script focuses it again right after the move. Soft keyboard open → `hidden`. Each phone view keeps 88 (`--ip-voice-clear`) under its content and in `scroll-padding-bottom` (+ the bar), and a phone Toast sits `space-2` above the key (amends FIN02 §48).
- **Shortcut:** Ctrl + Shift + Space starts / stops from anywhere without moving focus; Esc cancels voice before it closes a SidePanel, the S16 sheet or a menu.
- **Preview:** the states 1:1 (phone and desktop), the six phone placements (tab bar, SaveBar, Toast above the key, over a BottomSheet, keyboard open: the phone keyboard drawn over the tab bar and no key, after a cancel, the S16 sheet foot) and the desktop header with the executat / export Toasts. Built by `design/system/scripts/vo01_build.py`, checked by `vo01_render.py`.
