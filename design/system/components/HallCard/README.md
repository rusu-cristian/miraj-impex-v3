# HallCard (project addition, SYS05)

At a laying farm the Zilnic form groups compartments by hall, and the hall carries the Ouă entry (spec C1 step 3; EGG_PRODUCTION is recorded on the parent section). CSS in additions.css §13.

- **Markup:** `section.ip-hall[aria-labelledby]` > `header.ip-hall__head` (on the ground, `line-strong` top edge like SectionTotals: `ip-cap` "Hală", `h3.ip-hall__code` "H1", `ip-note` "secțiuni H1.1 · H1.2", right: SexCount pair alive at the start of the day, hidden "în viață") > `.ip-hall__sections` (its EntryCards, see EntryCardPlus) > the Ouă group. Several halls stack in `.ip-halls` (32px apart). Growth farms have no HallCard (sections are cards on their own).
- **Ouă group:** `section.ip-panel.ip-eggs` > `.ip-eggs__head` (RecordTile `ip-rec--eggs` egg icon, `aria-hidden`; `h4` "Ouă · H1"; `ip-note` "pe hală, pentru H1.1 + H1.2"; Tag on the right when saved) > `.ip-eggs__grid`: CountInputs (no sex bar) Jos, Cat. 1, Cat. 2, Cat. 3, Incubabile (`inputmode=numeric`, whole numbers; value digits only, grouping on blur as in EntryCardPlus) and TextField "Masa medie" (`inputmode=decimal`, suffix g, decimal comma; the label carries a hidden `ip-sr` " grame"), drawn at the CountInput height (`.ip-eggs__weight`) so the rows stay equal. Empty = "–" (null, not 0). Phone: 2 per row; wide: 6 in a row.
- **Computed, read-only** `dl.ip-eggs__calc` (not a live region): **Total ouă** = Cat. 1 + Cat. 2 + Cat. 3 + Incubabile (D18), sub-line "= Cat. 1 + 2 + 3 + Incubabile · Jos separat (345)"; shown only when all four are filled (0 counts), else "–" "completează cele 4 câmpuri". **Ouat azi** = total ÷ hens alive at the start of the day, one decimal, "75,8%"; sub-line "ghid 74,1% · +1,7 puncte · din ♀ 8 556" ("din ♀ 8 556" in `span.ip-eggs__base`, kept on one line; the sub-line wraps only between segments, FIN15); Lamp `ok` "în normă" within ±3 points, `warn` "abatere" beyond (D19, sub-line adds "(prag ±3)"). Empty: "–" with "după ce introduci ouăle", no Lamp. No guide: "ghid: – fără date în ghid", no Lamp (D29). Values Plex Mono 16/20 500 `ink`.
- **States:** gol (all "–") · completat · abatere on lay (H3 70,1% vs 74,1%, −4,0 puncte) · salvat (read-only, Tag "salvat", foot "Salvat la 08:35 · Dr. Ceban" / "poți modifica până la 23:59" + Editează). A count above what makes sense isn't refused (no rule in C1); Total ouă greater than 0 with no hens alive is not reachable (no card without birds).
- **Layouts:** phone (default): one column, section cards then Ouă. `ip-hall--wide` (desktop, for content ≥ ~640px): section cards in 2 columns, the Ouă group across both with 6 fields in a row and the two computed figures side by side; controls 32px (`ip-count--md`). Narrow fallback: `.ip-hall__sections` uses `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(100%, calc(var(--space-12) * 6)), 1fr))` (cards ≥ 288px, one column below that) and `.ip-eggs__grid` the same with `calc(var(--space-12) * 2)` (fields ≥ 96px; 3 per row at 358px).
- **Tokens:** existing only (`panel`, `line`, `line-strong`, `rule`, `rec-eggs(-bg)`, `rec-hen`, `ok`/`warn` lamps, `ink*`, `space-2…8`); `--ip-fs-alloc` (16/20) for the computed values.
- **Preview figures:** sample 16.11.2026 (D27): H1 Jos 345, Cat. 1 23, Cat. 2 131, Cat. 3 70, Incubabile 6 264, Masa medie 68,3 g → total 6 488, ouat 75,8% (÷ ♀ 8 556) vs ghid SAPT 40 74,1%; H3 115 / 16 / 85 / 47 / 5 792, 70,1 g → 5 940, 70,1% (÷ ♀ 8 475) abatere.
