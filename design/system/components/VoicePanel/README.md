# VoicePanel

What the app hears and does with a voice command, and a typed command (spec §8 F1, D199). additions.css §78 (VO01); spec components.md › Additions › VO00 / VO01.

- **Phone:** the BottomSheet: `div.ip-scrim.ip-scrim--sheet[data-state] > section.ip-sheet.ip-voicepanel` (handle, head, body, foot), up to 60% of the screen, the body scrolls; the floating VoiceButton docks in the foot. Sheet motion (§75: rises 280, scrim fades). A tap on the scrim cancels.
- **Desktop:** `section.ip-voicepanel.ip-voicepanel--pop[data-state]` inside `span.ip-voiceanchor`, under the header key: right edges aligned, `space-2` below it, `panel-w`, `panel` + 1px `line` + `radius-md` + `shadow-overlay`; menu motion: scale 0.95 + opacity from its top-right corner, 120. Closes on Esc, Închide, a click outside, or after a run that navigates.
- **Not modal:** `role=region` named "Comandă vocală" (its h2); focus moves into it only when a clarification, an error or an answer needs a choice.
- **Parts:** head (h2 · `p.ip-voicepanel__state[role=status]` = the panel's one polite region: Lamp + word "Ascult…" ok / "Mă gândesc…" info / "aștept permisiunea" info / "oprit" off, an answer or a question as hidden text · `.ip-voicepanel__time` Mono "0:04", outside the region · Închide) · body: `.ip-voicepanel__level` (`div.ip-voicemeter[aria-hidden][data-level=0–12]` + hint) · transcript `p.ip-voicepanel__said` (`.is-partial` = `ink-secondary`) or, once final, `button.ip-voicepanel__said` (pencil, described "Apasă pe text ca să-l corectezi"): tapped, the run stops and its text goes into the foot field (Execută primary, Anulează beside it; phone: under it) · `p.ip-voicepanel__cmd` (normalised command, Mono small, ♀/♂ signs) · `.ip-voicepanel__result` (answer + `p.ip-voicepanel__opened` "Am deschis …" + Anulează; clarification `div.ip-chips.ip-voicechips[role=radiogroup]` named by the question, ≤ 3 `button.ip-chip.ip-voicechip[role=radio]` + "Alta…", touch size and `ip-chips--fill` on phone; Banners: info refusal, warn fără microfon, the §49 failure Banner with Reîncearcă, all `role=alert`) · `.ip-voicepanel__ex` ("Poți spune:" + 2–4 `button.ip-btn--ghost.ip-voiceex`, 48 rows with a rule on phone) · foot: the one field "Scrie comanda" (`span.ip-input.ip-voicepanel__text > textarea rows=1`, 1–3 lines, grows, Enter runs it) + Execută (desktop a Button; phone a 48 square key `.ip-voicepanel__run`, corner-down-left icon, aria-label "Execută"; no paper-plane icon) + on phone the docked key.
- **Level meter:** 12 segments `space-1` × `space-4`, `space-1` apart, `sunken` track; lit `ink-secondary`, dark `line` (the spec's `rule` is 1.04:1 on `sunken`, so the 12 slots did not show; VO01). A readout, not an animation: the script writes `data-level` from the microphone at most 15 times a second; no transition, no easing, unchanged under reduced motion; stopped = 0.
- **States drawn (390 + 1440):** cerere permisiune, ascultă (start with examples; live transcript), oprire automată (20 s cap; pause), transcriere · se gândește, transcriere corectată, răspuns, clarificare (+ "Alta…" desktop), neînțeles (incl. "Nu am auzit nimic."), nepermis (D77 refusal; the never list), fără microfon (Chrome phone, iPhone Safari, home-screen app, desktop; no device), fără conexiune / eroare and offline before start, nepermis also "Nu ai acces la această fermă." and "Parolele nu se spun cu voce.". Transcripts are the words as heard; figures appear in the parsed line and the results. Executat, precompletat and anulare close the panel: VoiceButton and VoiceMark previews.
- **Demo:** the preview's first block steps through the flow with a small script (the real key and popover, the meter written from a sample level, auto-stop after the phrase, thinking, the executat Toast; Esc (a document capture listener while open), Închide, a click outside or a tap while thinking cancels; the status region is written only when its text changes; Ctrl+Shift+Space keeps focus; a reduced-motion switch). The canvas board V1 · Voce draws the states static.
