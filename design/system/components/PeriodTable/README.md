# PeriodTable

A DataTable laid out by the birds' age week with its days inside (D154, D159): every tab of the scope pages (S3 Serie, S4 Fermă, S5 Secțiune) and the S11 Consum / S14 Producție reports. Spec: `design/kit/components.md` › Additions › PeriodTable (round 2, spec). CSS: `components/additions.css` §71 (SYS14), on top of the DataTable rules §32 (row heads, totals edge, head icon), §36 (scroll region), §42 (skeleton rows), §54 (column shares), §69/§70 (one row height). Tokens only.

## Anatomy
- **Head controls** (farm tabs only, not Rezumat, S3, S5 or the reports): `div.ip-ptable__bar` right above the region: `span.ip-cap` "Detaliu" + SegmentedControl Fermă · Hale · Compartimente (`span.ip-seg[role=radiogroup][aria-labelledby]`, `ip-seg--touch` on a phone). Then the visible caption line `p.ip-cap.ip-ptable__cap[aria-hidden]` ("Păsări · Hîrbovăț · Seria 2026 · SAPT 56–59 · pe hale").
- **Region:** `div.ip-tablescroll.ip-tablescroll--framed[role=region][tabindex=0][aria-label=caption]` > `table.ip-table.ip-table--rowheads.ip-ptable` with `caption.ip-sr`. The head sticks to the region's top and the first column to its start; the page caps the region with `--ip-ptable-max-h` (viewport less its own sticky header); unset (boards) the region is uncapped.
- **Head:** up to three rows, `th[scope=col|colgroup]` in the caption face: section ("Total fermă", "Hala 5" …, note line `span.ip-ptable__note` "goală din 14.11") > record (`span.ip-table__ico.ip-rec.ip-rec--<type>` + word; "În viață" has no icon) > leaf. A record with a sex and several measures uses **combined leaves** ("♀ efectiv", "♀ ghid", "♂ efectiv"): the sign in `span.is-hen` / `span.is-rooster` + hidden "găini"/"cocoși" (`ip-sr`). A record with one column is a single head spanning the leaf row. Section, group and leaf heads are centred over their columns (D175); figures stay right-aligned, the spine left-aligned. A table with one section drops the section row.
- **Column groups (SYS15, D175):** every cell of a record group (head, leaves, week, day, foot and skeleton cells) carries its tint `ip-ptable__ga` | `ip-ptable__gb`, alternating in table order across the sections (neighbours never share one); its first column also `ip-ptable__gs`, a full-height `colgroup-rule` left edge from the head to the foot (a section's first column keeps `.ip-ptable__sec`, same edge); on Hale / Compartimente tables a hall's or section's start edge is `line-strong` (halls separate from groups through the body); the region's frame is the last group's right edge. Tints: day cells `colgroup-a|b`, week rows and the foot `colgroup-a|b-total` (one step over the body, so week rows keep their stronger look with the 500 weight and the `line-strong` top edge), head cells `colgroup-a|b-head`; A sand from `rec-feed` (9 / 11 / 13 %), B cool from `rec-weighing` (8 / 10 / 12 %) on `steel-0`, each body ≥1.12:1 against white. Leaf heads are centred over right-aligned figures (the user's request, D175; an exception to craft §5). The spine, the section heads and spanning message rows stay untinted (a spanning row interrupts the rules). Forced colours: tints drop, rules stay (CanvasText).
- **Spine:** Data (`th.ip-ptable__data`, sticky, takes the slack) · Vârstă (zile) · SAPT (Mono, right). S3 adds Fermă (text) after SAPT.
- **Week block:** `tbody.ip-ptable__week` per age week, newest first. Week row `tr.is-total.ip-ptable__wk` (totals look: 500, `panel`, `line-strong` top edge) > `th[scope=rowgroup]` > `button.ip-ptable__key[aria-expanded][aria-controls=<day row ids>]`: chevron-right / chevron-down 16 `ink-secondary` + "SAPT 58" + dates `span.ip-ptable__dates` ("· 10.11–16.11", `ink-secondary`; a partial week adds "· 2 din 7 zile"). Visible label `aria-hidden`, name in `ip-sr`: "SAPT 58, 10.11–16.11.2026, 7 zile". Vârstă shows "399–405", SAPT "58"; figures = sums, end values, week rates.
- **Daily rows:** `tr.ip-ptable__day[id]` (`hidden` while closed), `th[scope=row]` "Ma 17.11" (weekday `aria-hidden` + full weekday in `ip-sr`), indented to the week label (`space-8`), 400 on `raised`.
- **Foot:** `tfoot > tr.is-total` "Total perioadă": sums, end values at the period's end, rates over the period (guide rates weighted by hen-days / bird-days, D162); Vârstă "385–407", SAPT "56–59". Its first cell sticks with the first column (rule edge, `panel`).
- **Below:** `div.ip-ptable__after` (S3 key, a partial guide's foot note).

## Props
- `grain`: `day` (default, D166: every week of the period open, its week row followed by its day rows, newest first; keys `aria-expanded="true"`, each can close its week) | `week` (week rows only, every week closed). Opening or closing one week never changes the grain. Builder: `ptable(open_weeks=None)` = every week open; `open_weeks=()` = grain Săpt.
- `sections` (Detaliu): `farm` | `halls` | `compartments` (each section repeats the record groups after "Total fermă").
- `tags`: column groups: "În viață" first, right after the spine (always on Păsări and Rezumat), then the record groups in RecordCode order, "Vânzare" always last (D167); a type with no records in the period has no columns. Mortalitate = ♀ · ♂ deaths, then its own group "% cumulat" (mortality icon) with plain ♀ · ♂ leaves, one decimal: real deaths since arrival ÷ birds arrived, sales not counted (D155, D162; 5,7% · 34,7% on 18.11.2026). It shows on week, day and foot rows: the day rows go beyond the spec ("week row also"), accepted under D162. Producție: Găini (week = hens at the week's start, D162) · Ouat ouă · % ouat · ghid · față de ghid (pct) · stare (Lamp + word: "în normă" within ±3 pct, else "abatere") · Incubabile · Jos · Masă ou.
- `guide`: `full` | `partial` (`–` + hidden "fără date în ghid", foot note) | `none` (ghid / față de ghid / stare columns removed, D92; page header Tag "fără ghid").
- `density`: default | `dense` (`ip-ptable--dense`: figure cells `space-2` inline, S3 at 1280).
- `weighings` (Greutate): open weeks list only the days with a weighing; a week without one keeps its key and opens to one muted row `tr.ip-ptable__day.ip-ptable__none > td[colspan=all] > span` "fără cântărire în această săptămână" (`ink-muted`, from the day labels' start, sticky there in a scrolled region; D167), its week-row cells `–` + hidden "fără cântărire".
- `allWeeks` (S3): the last 4 weeks, then a secondary Button "Arată toate săptămânile" in `ip-ptable__after` with `aria-controls` = the table id (then "Arată ultimele 4 săptămâni"); focus stays on it.
- `rowGroups` (S11 Consum, S14): after a week's day rows, the section week rows as `th.ip-table__child` (§32).
- **Phone** (`ip-ptable--phone`, phone markup): spine folded into the sticky first column (head "Data · Vârstă (zile)", days "Ma 17.11 · 406", weeks "SAPT 58" with "10.11–16.11" on a second small line, `span.ip-ptable__sub`; a partial week's second line is "2 din 7 zile", D162/D171); every body and foot row `control-h-touch` (48), key 44; cells tighter (figures `space-1` | `space-2`, row heads and head cells `space-2`, head words wrap), so one tag's group fits 358 px (D171); a companion rate as a second small Mono line in the same cell (`span.ip-ptable__pct`, Mortalitate "10" / "5,7%", head note "cu % cumulat"); one column group at a time (single-choice chips per tab in the FilterBar sheet, default = the tab's own type; S3 shows every group); what is still wider scrolls sideways.

## States
Loaded · loading (same table and head, `tr.ip-table__skel` rows with `ip-skel` bars, week-row bars in `rule`; region `aria-hidden`, block `aria-busy`, hidden `role=status` line; bars `ip-skel--chN` as wide as each column's usual figure, plus the loaded `--ip-colw` shares on the columns whose figures outgrow their head, §54; loading vs loaded ≤0.3 px at 1440) · no data in the period (EmptyState in `ip-emptybox` in place of the region, no Detaliu bar, D48) · no guide / partial guide · partial current week ("2 din 7 zile", open by default) · missing day (`–` + Lamp "lipsă") · emptied section (`–` + hidden reason after its last day, group note "goală din 14.11", D158) · emptying weeks (Vânzare beside real deaths, D155) · load error (§49 failure Banner with Reîncearcă in place of the region).

## A11y
Detaliu radiogroup before the region in the focus order; real table semantics (caption, `thead` with `scope=col|colgroup`, one `tbody` per week = row group, week `th[scope=rowgroup]`, day `th[scope=row]`); keys are native buttons (Enter/Space toggle, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` = the day rows), focus ring 2px `focus` + 2px offset, the focused header rises above its neighbours; closed day rows `hidden`; `–` cells carry their reason as hidden text; focus order region → keys in row order. Contrast at 1440: dates and notes `ink-secondary` 7.9:1 on `panel`; on the group tints (SYS15) ♀ ≥5.2:1, ♂ ≥5.4:1, ink ≥14.8:1, head captions ≥5.2:1, lamp words ≥5.2:1, lamp dots ≥3.6:1, focus ring ≥5.1:1.
